# Proactive Contract Renewal — Voiceover Script
**Target runtime: 3:30** (~500 words at a measured demo pace)

---

**[0:00–0:15] — The problem**

*(Visual: Overview tab)*

Every year, Carrier's Climate Solutions Transportation business renews thousands of HVAC service contracts across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Today, that process is reactive — a churn risk usually surfaces only after it's already too late to act on it.

**[0:15–0:45] — The shift to proactive**

*(Visual: "Run daily batch" button, milestone cards)*

This is a proactive renewal system, not just a prediction tool. Every day, it scans the book of business, and the moment a contract crosses a renewal milestone — 90, 60, 45, 30, or 10 days out — it automatically triggers a fresh recommendation. No one has to go looking for risk. The system brings it to them, at exactly the right moment.

**[0:45–1:15] — Renewal Prioritization**

*(Visual: Renewal Prioritization tab, bucket cards, quadrant chart)*

This is the Renewal Prioritization view — the account manager's home base. Contracts are bucketed by days-to-expiry, filterable by region and channel. And here's the risk-versus-value quadrant: every contract plotted by its risk score against its contract value. The accounts in the top right — high risk *and* high value — are the ones that matter most, and they're the ones the system surfaces first.

**[1:15–1:45] — Prioritized worklist**

*(Visual: Portfolio KPIs, sortable worklist table)*

Below that, portfolio KPIs update live with every filter — contracts needing action, revenue at risk, converted value, response rate. And the worklist itself is fully sortable, so a rep can work top-down by risk, by value, or by region. Click any contract, and its full detail opens.

**[1:45–2:20] — Inside a contract**

*(Visual: Contract detail drawer — AI summary, recommendation, draft email)*

Here, the AI has already read the service history, the payment pattern, the sentiment in past feedback — and summarized the whole relationship in plain language. It recommends one specific action — a personal outreach call, a loyalty offer, a service check-in — with the reasoning behind it. Every recommendation is quality-checked by a second AI pass before it ever reaches a human; anything that doesn't meet the bar gets escalated for review instead of guessed at. And it drafts the actual outreach email, ready to send straight from the app.

**[2:20–2:45] — Closing the loop**

*(Visual: Log outcome buttons)*

Once the rep reaches out, they log what happened — Engaged, Declined, or No response — right there. That outcome feeds straight back into the system, so every campaign becomes measurable, and the portal becomes the single source of truth for what's actually working.

**[2:45–3:10] — Rolling it up**

*(Visual: Dashboard tab — global/regional cards, campaign performance, Trace & Metrics)*

Zoom out to the Dashboard, and the same engine rolls up globally and by region — North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific — showing which retention plays are driving real renewals, in dollars. And for full transparency, every AI decision is traceable: the prompts, the scores, the retries, right down to the cost per recommendation.

**[3:10–3:30] — Close**

*(Visual: Overview tab, five-step value proposition)*

One engine. Three regions. From spotting risk, to recommending action, to proving what worked — this turns contract renewal from something the business reacts to, into something it drives.
