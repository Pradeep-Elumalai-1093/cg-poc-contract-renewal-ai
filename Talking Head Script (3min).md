# Proactive Contract Renewal — Talking Head Script
**Target runtime: ~3:10** (~420 words, presenter to camera, cutting to screen where noted)

---

**[0:00–0:19] — To camera**

Every year, Carrier's Climate Solutions business renews thousands of HVAC service contracts across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Every day, in the background, the system scans that book and automatically generates fresh renewal recommendations for the accounts that need them.

**[0:19–1:22] — Screen: Renewal Prioritization tab, filters applied**

Now, lets look at the Renewal Prioritization screen — an account manager's home base. Filtering by region, channel, or expiry bucket updates the entire page at once — the quadrant chart, the KPI cards, the campaign-response chart, all of it, so you set the view once and everything on the page matches it. The quadrant plots every contract by risk score against contract value; up here, top right, high risk and high value, are the accounts that matter most. Down here, low risk but high value — that's your low-hanging fruit, easy to upsell without much risk. And it's zoomable, so you can pop it open full-screen. The KPI cards cover revenue at risk, converted value, and response rate. And right alongside it, a campaign-response chart broken out by risk bucket, so you can see whether outreach lands better with high-risk accounts or lower-risk ones.

**[1:22–1:43] — Screen: worklist**

Scroll down to the worklist — fully searchable & sortable, so a rep works straight down the list. It's built around the real relationship: one customer can have several contracts, and you can filter down to exactly the group you need using the contract expiry buckets up top.

**[1:43–2:19] — Screen: contract detail panel opens**

Click into a contract, and the recommendation is right up front. The AI's already read the service history, the payment pattern, the sentiment in past feedback, and summarized the relationship in plain language. It recommends one specific campaign action, with the reasoning, and drafts the outreach email — ready to go. And every recommendation gets a second AI review before a human sees it.

**[2:19–2:26] — Screen: Send email**

Hitting send opens Outlook with the email already drafted — no copying, no pasting.

**[2:26–2:42] — Screen: Log outcome**

Reps log the outcome — engaged, declined, no response. And a no-response doesn't disappear — it feeds forward, so at the next milestone the AI already knows what was tried, and recommends something different.

**[2:42–3:00] — Screen: Dashboard tab, campaign breakdown**

Navigating to Dashboard, the same picture rolled up by globally and by region, plus a full campaign breakdown. For each type of campaign, you can see how many accounts were assigned, engaged, declined, or gave no-response and what that means in real dollars, so you see exactly which play is converting.

**[3:00–3:07] — To camera**

One engine, three regions. From spotting risk, to recommending action, to proving what worked.
