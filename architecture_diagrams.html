1. Problem statement

The brief this system was built to answer, as given.

  • Support proactive contract renewal with margin and customer context.
  • Identifies customers eligible for contract renewal or at risk for non-renewal.
  • It recommends proactive retention actions + campaigns.

2. Business process flow

Who does what, from an active contract through to a renewed, renegotiated, or lost outcome. The AI System lane is deliberately collapsed to one block — see section 5 for the detailed agent mechanics.

↕ Drag any box to rearrange

The daily batch job runs every day, but a given contract-milestone is only processed once — the next run only fires when the contract crosses into a new threshold (e.g. 90→60 days), not on every daily run. The feedback loop (dashed amber/red) is a fixed path and does not follow drag movement.
