# Proactive Contract Renewal — Talking Head Script
**Target runtime: 3:00** (~400 words, presenter speaking to camera, cutting to screen where noted)

---

**[0:00–0:20] — To camera**

Every year, Carrier's Climate Solutions business renews thousands of HVAC service contracts, across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. And today, that process is reactive. By the time someone notices an account is about to walk, it's often already too late to do anything about it.

**[0:20–0:45] — To camera, cut to app**

*[Screen: app opens, "Run daily batch" button]*

So we built something different — a proactive renewal system. Every day, it scans the entire book of business, and the moment a contract crosses a renewal milestone — 90 days out, 60, 45, 30, down to 10 — it automatically triggers a recommendation. Nobody has to go hunting for risk. It comes to them.

**[0:45–1:15] — Screen: Renewal Prioritization tab**

This is the Renewal Prioritization screen — an account manager's home base. Contracts are bucketed by days to expiry, and filterable by region and channel. And this chart right here is the heart of it: every contract, plotted by risk score against contract value. The ones up here, in the top right — high risk *and* high value — those are the accounts that matter most, and they're exactly what the system surfaces first.

**[1:15–1:40] — Screen: KPIs and worklist**

Below that, portfolio KPIs update live as you filter — revenue at risk, converted value, response rate. And the worklist is fully sortable, so a rep can just work straight down the list. Click any contract, and everything about it opens up.

**[1:40–2:10] — Screen: contract detail drawer**

And here's where the AI actually does the work. It's already read the service history, the payment pattern, the sentiment in past feedback — and it hands you a plain-language summary of the relationship. Then it recommends one specific action, with the reasoning behind it, and drafts the actual outreach email — ready to send. And every one of those recommendations gets a second AI review before a human ever sees it, so anything shaky gets flagged for a person to check, instead of just being sent out.

**[2:10–2:30] — To camera, cut to screen**

Once the rep reaches out, they log what happened — engaged, declined, no response — right there in the app. That feeds straight back into the system, so this becomes the one place everyone looks to know what's actually working.

**[2:30–2:50] — Screen: Dashboard tab**

Zoom out to the Dashboard, and you get the same view rolled up globally and by region, showing which retention plays are actually driving renewals — in real dollars. And every AI decision behind it is fully traceable, down to the prompt and the cost.

**[2:50–3:00] — To camera**

One engine, three regions. From spotting the risk, to recommending the action, to proving what worked.
